Last updated: September 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact lists.

B. Downloaded Content & Local Storage

  • Any videos, files, or media downloaded using the App are stored strictly and locally on your device in your designated storage directory.
  • We do not upload, monitor, store, or have access to the videos you download or watch.
  • Your download history and search queries remain stored strictly on your device using encrypted local preferences and can be cleared at any time in Settings.

C. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob to support free development and maintenance.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable device information in accordance with its privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data (country/city level)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded video views)
  • Diagnostic and crash logs for ad delivery optimization

For more details on how Google uses information from partner apps, please visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

2. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only the minimal permissions required to function properly.

INTERNET Purpose: Allows fetching video streams, downloading files, and loading Google AdMob ads.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / Storage Purpose: Enables accessing and playing downloaded videos on your device.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Purpose: Displays real-time download progress percentages, speeds, and completion notices in your status bar.
WAKE_LOCK Purpose: Keeps the CPU active during active file transfers so long downloads are not interrupted when the screen turns off.

The App does not request or use location, camera, microphone, contacts, or phone state permissions.

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes trusted third-party SDKs:

Google Play Services Purpose: For core system integration and app updates.

All downloaded files and preferences remain strictly within your device's local sandbox or public media storage under your direct control.
