Privacy Policy for MindGlow

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to MindGlow ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing a peaceful, uplifting experience for your daily mindfulness practice.

Quick Summary: MindGlow does not require accounts or collect your name, phone number, or email. Your saved favorites remain stored directly on your phone. We use Google AdMob for optional advertising.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information: We do not collect or request personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or contact list.

B. On-Device Storage: Preferences and saved favorite quotes are stored locally on your device via shared preferences. They never leave your device.

C. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display rewarded and banner ads. Google AdMob may process non-personal diagnostic metrics and Advertising IDs in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

2. App Permissions

MindGlow requests minimal standard permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load Google AdMob advertisements and check for Play Store in-app updates.

3. Children's Privacy

MindGlow is designed for general audiences seeking mindfulness. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any such data has inadvertently reached us, please contact us immediately.

4. Security

Because we do not store personal accounts or sensitive databases remotely, your personal data is not exposed to third-party database breaches. Third-party SDK network calls utilize encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.
