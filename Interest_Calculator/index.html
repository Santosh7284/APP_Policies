Our Commitment to Privacy

Interestly - Interest Calculator ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Sathcodec. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our app and how it is used.

Interestly does not collect, store, or transmit the financial information you enter. All calculations are performed entirely on your device.
📊
Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information.
  • Financial information such as principal amount, interest rate, or calculation values.
  • Saved scenarios or portfolio information beyond your own device.
  • Precise location information.
  • Personal identifiers beyond what Google AdMob may require.
📱
Data Stored on Your Device

Interestly stores your saved scenarios and preferences locally on your Android device using standard local storage (such as SharedPreferences and local files).

  • Data never leaves your device.
  • We cannot access your saved information.
  • Data is removed when you uninstall the app.
📣
Advertising (Google AdMob)

Interestly displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information to provide and improve advertising services.

Examples include:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device type and operating system
  • Language settings
  • IP address for approximate location
  • Ad impressions and interaction data

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy

You can manage personalized advertising through your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

📄
Exported PDF & CSV Files

When you export a PDF or CSV statement:

  • The file is created entirely on your device.
  • It is saved only to your local storage.
  • Sharing occurs only through apps you choose.
  • We never receive or store exported files.

Children's Privacy

Interestly is rated Everyone and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe personal information has been provided by a child, please contact us and we will address the request promptly.
